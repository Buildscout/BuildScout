# Acceptance criteria

- DallasNow is represented as the current official Dallas permitting source.
- Legacy historical Dallas OpenData remains blocked from current production opportunities.
- Current-source records have a normalization and validation contract.
- Missing permit number/address/source evidence causes rejection.
- Demo/test fixtures cannot pass the production gate.
- Automated DallasNow retrieval remains off until a documented public or authorized feed is configured.
