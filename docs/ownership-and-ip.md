# BuildScout — Ownership & IP Control

## Owner
BuildScout is the user's project, product concept, brand, codebase, product design, documentation, and business.

This repository is proprietary project work unless a future written agreement expressly states otherwise.

## Contribution rule
No employee, contractor, freelancer, agency, partner, vendor, or collaborator should contribute code, designs, documentation, models, data-processing logic, or other original work to BuildScout without a written agreement that clearly assigns the applicable intellectual-property rights to the BuildScout owner/entity.

Before outside development begins, use a written invention/IP assignment and confidentiality agreement appropriate to the person's role and jurisdiction.

## Third-party material
Third-party libraries, APIs, datasets, trademarks, documents, plans, and other material remain owned by their respective rights holders and must be used only under applicable licenses, permissions, or legal rights.

BuildScout must not claim ownership of customer-uploaded plans or documents. Customer content remains subject to the customer's rights and the platform's terms.

## Security principles
- Owner-controlled GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, domain, email, payment, analytics, and AI/provider accounts.
- Enable MFA on every owner/admin account.
- Use separate production credentials and never commit secrets.
- Store secrets in Vercel/Supabase secret management, not source code.
- Use least-privilege access for collaborators.
- Keep production database backups and export/recovery procedures.
- Protect private project documents with authorization checks and storage policies.
- Keep an audit trail for important project, document, bid, and membership actions.
- Never expose private bids, private plans, customer data, API keys, or service-role credentials.

## Product/IP records
Maintain dated records of:
- product specifications
- architecture decisions
- original designs
- source code commits
- major releases
- business/product concepts
- brand usage
- domain ownership
- contractor assignments

This file is a project-control record. It is not a substitute for legal agreements or registrations.

## Required legal protection work
Before commercial scale, the owner should have a qualified U.S. attorney review:
1. entity ownership of BuildScout and all IP
2. founder/assignment documents
3. contractor and employee IP assignments
4. confidentiality/trade-secret controls
5. customer terms and acceptable-use rules
6. plan/document licensing and uploader warranties
7. privacy policy and data-processing obligations
8. trademark clearance and filing strategy
9. applicable construction-bidding and marketplace laws
