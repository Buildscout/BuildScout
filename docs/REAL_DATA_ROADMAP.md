# BuildScout Real Data Roadmap

## Gate 1 — Trust foundation
Source adapters, provenance validation, authoritative source identity, verification timestamps and production eligibility.

## Gate 2 — Dallas live ingestion
Connect an approved official Dallas source, ingest real records, preserve upstream IDs/URLs, deduplicate and expose source verification in the UI.

## Gate 3 — Documents
Discover and attach only publicly available official project documents/plans/spec links. Preserve document source and verification metadata. Never imply plans/specs exist when they are unavailable or access-restricted.

## Gate 4 — Texas expansion
Add adapters for additional high-value Texas jurisdictions and monitor source health.

## Gate 5 — United States
Prioritize major construction markets, then expand jurisdiction coverage nationally. BuildScout can support multiple municipal platform families behind one normalized project model.

## Gate 6 — Canada
Add Canadian jurisdiction/province adapters under the same provenance and verification contract.

## Gate 7 — Commercial product
Public marketing homepage, onboarding, subscription/billing, account limits, entitlements, source-health operations and production monitoring.
