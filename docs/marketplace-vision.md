# BuildScout Marketplace Vision

BuildScout is both a construction intelligence network and a project marketplace.

## Project owners / GCs
- Publish a project.
- Upload authorized plans, specifications, addenda, and other bid documents.
- Choose which trades are invited.
- Set bid due date.
- Review bidder submissions.
- Shortlist and award.
- Keep project intelligence, documents, and bidding in one workspace.

## Trades / installers
- Discover projects matching trade, geography, size, and stage.
- View documents they are authorized to access.
- Ask BuildScout AI to summarize scope and identify likely trade requirements.
- Submit bids/proposals.
- Track invitations, submissions, and outcomes.

## Suppliers / manufacturers
- Discover projects where their products or systems are relevant.
- See project stage, scope, specifications, and participating firms.
- Identify upcoming material demand before installation.
- Receive opportunity alerts.

## Architects / engineers
- Discover projects at planning/design stages.
- Publish or share authorized documents.
- Connect project teams and revisions.
- Find qualified contractors and specialty partners.

## AI layer
The AI should not invent project facts. It should ground summaries and recommendations in project records, documents, and source evidence, identify uncertainty, and link recommendations back to evidence.

## Business model direction
The marketplace can eventually support free project posting with paid intelligence, trade lead access, premium alerts, team seats, bid-management features, supplier/manufacturer intelligence, and enterprise plans. Do not lock the core marketplace behind monetization before product-market fit is demonstrated.

## Safety / rights
Only display or distribute plans and specifications that BuildScout is authorized to store/display. Preserve uploader, source, revision, access scope, and provenance. Private bid information is visible only to authorized project participants.
